Jay Law–inspired Momentum Rules · Quick Guide

1. Market regimeRED: no new trades
2. SetupUptrend + relative strength + volume + breakout / pullback
3. EntryOnly when setup confirms; never chase extended price
4. Stop lossTechnical invalidation level—not a fixed 0.5% price stop
5. Risk0.5% of account = ฿50 maximum planned loss per trade
6. Position sizeRisk budget ÷ (Entry − Stop) converted to THB
7. RewardPrefer ≥4R; default target 5R if chart permits
8. Portfolio≤5% aggregate open risk; ≤10 positions; ≥30% cash
9. LimitsDaily loss 1.5% (฿150); weekly 3% (฿300)
10. ProcessPaper trade; log rationale; review at 5% drawdown

These are the project’s risk controls inspired by the strategy—not a claim that every threshold is Jay Law’s official rule.
